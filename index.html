Our Solutions

U-Connect Core

U-Connect Core is the foundation of our solution, providing a secure, cloud-hosted platform for IDNOs to deliver Asset Adoption Valuations (AAVs) and manage interactions with UIPs, Developers, and ICPs.

It includes a sophisticated document library where all parties can upload, store, and retrieve project documents such as Design Packs and Groundworks Plans. A user hierarchy approval process ensures compliance by locking functionality to project milestones until key documents are uploaded.

Additionally, U-Connect Core offers enhanced reporting for improved efficiency and compliance, including Weekly Whereabouts, Site Breakdown, and Finance Reporting and Integration.

U-Connect OnSite

U-Connect OnSite is designed primarily for UIPs, Developers, and ICPs to manage daily onsite tasks, back-office operations, and streamline compliance.

Directly linked to U-Connect Core, OnSite makes it easy to request an Asset Adoption Value and, upon acceptance and contract approval, access and manage all project documentation.

It can also be used by contractors, EV Charge Point Installers, Battery Storage Installers, Data Centre Developers and other Commercial Developers who wish to contract directly with an IDNO.
